# How to use this guide

This is the practical companion to the research report. The report explains why; this guide says what to do each day and each week. The Shredded System app runs the same plan: it holds the meal templates, counts the carb-up clock, logs your weight and makes the weekly decision for you.

Read sections 1–4 once. After that you only need the daily routine (section 9) and the weekly check-in (section 10).

> **Before you start:** see a GP or prescriber about retatrutide and get baseline bloods (lipids, liver, kidney, glucose). This plan is not medical advice.

# 1. The plan on one page

| Area | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Diet base** | Gironda Cut: meat and eggs at every meal, green vegetables twice a day, carbs under 50 g on low days. |
| **Carb-up** | Every 72–96 hours: one day at about maintenance calories with 200–300 g carbs, protein unchanged, fat low. |
| **Calories** | Low days 400–600 kcal under maintenance. Never under 1,500 kcal without a clinician. |
| **Protein** | 160 g a day (150–190 g). Every day, hungry or not. |
| **Fibre** | 25–30 g a day: vegetables, berries, psyllium. |
| **Training** | Full Body A and B, twice a week, 1–2 sets, stop 1–2 reps short of failure on compounds. |
| **Steps** | Your current average + 1,500–2,000, building to 10,000–15,000. |
| **Rate** | 0.5–1.0% of bodyweight a week. |
| **Tracking** | Weigh daily, waist weekly, photos every 4 weeks. |
| **Decisions** | Once a week, from 14 days of data, one change at a time. |
| **Breaks** | 1–2 weeks at maintenance every 8–12 weeks. |
| **Never** | Dehydration tricks, raw eggs or milk, diuretics, under-eating protein, dose changes without a clinician. |

# 2. Phases

| Phase | Weeks | Weight | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0. Baseline** | Week 0 (now) | 95 kg | Log 7 days of normal eating and weight. Take photos, waist and a blood test. Set up the app. |
| **1. Gironda Cut I** | Weeks 1–10 | 95 → ~87 kg | Full plan. Expect a fast first two weeks (water). |
| **Diet break** | Weeks 11–12 | hold | Maintenance calories, Modern Gironda meals, same training. |
| **2. Gironda Cut II** | Weeks 13–22 | ~87 → ~80 kg | Add steps if the rate slows. |
| **Diet break** | Weeks 23–24 | hold | As before. |
| **3. Gironda Cut III** | Weeks 25–36 | ~80 → 75 kg | Rate slows to 0.5–0.7%. Protect strength. |
| **Decision point** | At 75 kg | 75 kg | Photos, waist and strength (or a DEXA scan). Continue to 70 kg only if visible fat remains and strength holds (report, section 7). |
| **4. Finish (optional)** | Weeks 37–50 | 75 → 70 kg | Slowest phase. Two carb-ups a week if training suffers. |
| **5. Maintenance** | After | goal | Add 100–200 kcal a week until weight is stable. Keep protein and training. |

# 3. Setting your numbers

## 3.1 Calories

**Step 1. Estimate maintenance.** Enter height, age, sex and activity in the app (Setup). It uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation:

- Men: (10 × weight in kg) + (6.25 × height in cm) − (5 × age) + 5
- Women: the same, − 161 instead of + 5

Multiply by activity: 1.2 if you sit most of the day, 1.375 if you're lightly active (most people who walk 8–10k steps), 1.55 if you're moderately active (12–15k steps and training).

**Step 2. Set the low-day target** at maintenance − 500 (range 400–600).

**Step 3. Correct it from real data after 14 days.** The app does this: average intake + (kg lost × 7,700 ÷ days) = your real maintenance.

Example (assumed values, replace with yours): a 180 cm, 40-year-old man at 95 kg, lightly active. BMR = 950 + 1,125 − 200 + 5 = 1,880 kcal. Maintenance ≈ 1,880 × 1.375 ≈ 2,585 kcal. Low-day target ≈ 2,085 kcal.

## 3.2 Protein, fat, carbs, fibre

| | Low day | Carb-up day |
|---|---|---|
| Calories | Maintenance − 500 | About maintenance |
| Protein | 160 g (150–190) | Same |
| Fat | 60–90 g | 40–50 g |
| Carbs | Under 50 g | 200–300 g |
| Fibre | 25–30 g | 30 g+ |
| Saturated fat | Under 25 g | Under 20 g |

## 3.3 Scaling the templates

The Gironda Cut template is 1,678 kcal. To reach your target, add in this order:

| Add | kcal | Protein | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 1 whole egg | 72 | 6 g | Easiest. |
| 50 g more steak or 5% beef | 62 | 11 g | Keeps it Gironda. |
| 10 g olive oil | 88 | 0 g | On vegetables. |
| 15 g almonds | 92 | 3 g | Snack or top-up. |
| 100 g more Greek yogurt | 54 | 10 g | Top-up. |

To go down, remove them in reverse order. Never drop protein below 150 g.

# 4. The day types

The app has four day types. Most weeks: 4 Gironda Cut days, 2 Modern Gironda (training) days and 1 carb-up.

## 4.1 Gironda Cut (default low day): 1,678 kcal

| Meal | Food | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| **Meal 1** (break the fast) | Whole eggs | 4 (200 g) |
| | 5% beef mince, shaped into patties | 250 g raw |
| | Spinach and mushrooms | 100 g each |
| | Butter, for cooking | 5 g |
| **Meal 2** | Rump steak, trimmed | 250 g raw |
| | Whole eggs | 2 |
| | Broccoli | 250 g |
| | Salad leaves and cucumber | 100 g |
| | Olive oil | 15 g (1½ tbsp) |
| **Top-up** (any time in the window) | Greek yogurt, 0% fat | 200 g |
| | Mixed berries | 100 g |
| | Almonds | 25 g |
| | Psyllium husk, with a large glass of water | 5 g (1 tsp) |

**Totals:** 1,678 kcal · 187 g protein · 85 g fat (25 g saturated) · 28 g carbs · 20 g fibre.

**How to cook:**

- Meal 1: shape the mince into 3 patties (about 80 g each), fry 4 minutes a side until cooked through. Wilt the spinach and mushrooms in the same pan with the butter. Fry or scramble the eggs last.
- Meal 2: take the steak out 20 minutes early, pat it dry, salt it, sear 2–4 minutes a side in a very hot pan and rest it for 5 minutes. Steam the broccoli for 5 minutes. Fry the eggs while the steak rests.

## 4.2 Strict Gironda (your current two meals): 1,865 kcal

| Meal | Food | kcal | Protein | Fat | Carbs |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Meal 1 | 6 eggs + 3 pork patties | 933 | 77 g | 66 g | 5 g |
| Meal 2 | 6 eggs + 250 g sirloin | 932 | 93 g | 60 g | 2 g |

**Totals:** 1,865 kcal · 170 g protein · 126 g fat (47 g saturated) · 7 g carbs · **0 g fibre**.

Use this when you can't cook vegetables (travel, a chaotic day). If you use it more than 2 days a week, fix the fibre at minimum: add 2 handfuls of salad and 5–10 g of psyllium, and swap the pork patties for 5% beef.

## 4.3 Modern Gironda (training day): 1,628 kcal

| Meal | Food | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| **Meal 1** | 3 eggs, 200 g egg whites, 100 g spinach | |
| | 60 g oats with 100 g berries | |
| **Meal 2** (after training) | 250 g rump steak, 400 g boiled potatoes, 200 g broccoli, salad, 10 g olive oil | |
| **Top-up** | 200 g Greek yogurt + 1 scoop whey | |

**Totals:** 1,628 kcal · 164 g protein · 44 g fat · 131 g carbs · 22 g fibre.

The carbs sit around training to keep sessions strong. If you prefer to stay Gironda-strict on training days too, use the Gironda Cut day and move the potatoes to the carb-up.

## 4.4 Carb-up day: 2,224 kcal

| Meal | Food |
|---|---|
| **Meal 1** | 3 eggs, 200 g egg whites, 80 g oats, 1 banana, 100 g berries |
| **Meal 2** | 250 g rump steak, 350 g cooked white rice, 200 g green beans |
| **Meal 3** | 200 g chicken breast, 350 g baked sweet potato, 200 g broccoli, 5 g olive oil |

**Totals:** 2,224 kcal · 180 g protein · 42 g fat · 267 g carbs · 35 g fibre.

**The carb-up clock:**

- Gironda's interval was about 72 hours. Use **72–96 hours** (every third or fourth day). The app counts it and turns the Today screen gold when one is due.
- Put the carb-up on a training day or the day before one.
- If weight has stalled for 2 weeks and you feel fine, stretch the interval to 120 hours. If training feels flat, shorten it to 72.
- Weight will jump 0.5–1.5 kg the next morning. That is glycogen and water. It's gone within 2–3 days.
- It isn't a cheat day. If you go off plan, log it honestly and carry on.

# 5. Eating window and fasting

The 18:6 window from your Trainer app (09:00–15:00) fits this plan: Meal 1 when the window opens, Meal 2 mid-window, the top-up before it closes.

- Fasting doesn't burn extra fat on its own; it is just a way to control intake. If the window makes protein hard to hit, widen it to 16:8.
- **No extended fasts (over 24 hours) while taking retatrutide** or any appetite-suppressing medication, unless a clinician has agreed.
- Drink 2.5–3.5 litres of water a day. Tea and black coffee are fine (your no-coffee rule is your choice, not a diet requirement).

## 6.2 Prep (Dolce style)

Twice a week (Sunday and Wednesday):

1. Shape all the beef mince into 80 g patties. Cook half, keep half raw in the fridge (cooked patties keep 3 days).

3. Portion yogurt, berries and almonds into tubs for the top-ups.
4. Cook the carb-up rice and potatoes the night before.

# 7. Training

## 7.1 The week

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Monday | **Full Body A** |
| Tuesday | Brisk walk, 25–40 minutes |
| Wednesday | Walk or easy cycling |
| Thursday | **Full Body B** (carb-up the day before or on the day) |
| Friday | Brisk walk, 25–40 minutes |
| Saturday | Longer easy walk |
| Sunday | Rest or a gentle walk |

## 7.2 Full Body A

| Exercise | Sets | Reps | Effort |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Leg press or safety-bar squat | 2 | 6–10 | 1–2 reps in reserve |
| Incline machine or dumbbell press | 2 | 6–10 | 1–2 in reserve |
| Neutral or underhand pulldown | 2 | 6–10 | 1–2 in reserve |
| Seated leg curl | 1–2 | 8–12 | 0–1 in reserve |
| Lateral raise | 1–2 | 10–15 | 0–1 in reserve |
| Calf raise | 1–2 | 10–15 | 0–1 in reserve |

## 7.3 Full Body B

| Exercise | Sets | Reps | Effort |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Romanian deadlift or machine hip hinge | 2 | 6–10 | 2 in reserve, never to failure |
| Chest-supported row | 2 | 6–10 | 1–2 in reserve |
| Machine chest press or assisted dip | 2 | 6–10 | 1–2 in reserve |
| Split squat or leg extension | 1–2 | 8–12 | 1 in reserve |
| Curl | 1 | 8–12 | 0–1 in reserve |
| Pressdown | 1 | 8–12 | 0–1 in reserve |
| Cable crunch or plank | 2 | 10–15 / 30–60 s | 1 in reserve |

## 7.4 Progression

- **Reps in reserve (RIR):** how many more good reps you could have done. RIR 1 = one more was possible.
- When you hit the top of the rep range on every set at the target RIR, add 2.5–5% to the weight next time.
- In a deficit, holding your weights is a win. Losing reps in two comparable sessions in a row is a warning sign (section 10).
- **Deload** every 8–10 weeks or when strength falls twice: same weights, half the sets, for one week. Line it up with a diet break if you can.
- **Mentzer option:** if you prefer your Trainer HIT programmes, keep them. Use one set to failure only on machines and isolation exercises.

## 7.5 Steps

Start at your current 7-day average plus 1,500–2,000 a day. Build toward 10,000–15,000. Your Trainer step chain (15,000 on 5 days, or 75,000 a week) is a good end point. Walking is the safest cardio in a long cut: it burns calories without eating into recovery.

# 8. Measuring progress

| What | When | How |
|---|---|---|
| Weight | Every morning | After the toilet, before food or drink, same scale. Only the 7-day average matters. |
| Waist | Monday | Tape at the navel, relaxed, after breathing out. |
| Photos | Every 4 weeks | Same light, same place, front, side and back. |
| Strength | Each session | Top-set weight and reps in the app. |
| Blood test | Start, then about every 12 weeks | Lipids, liver, kidney, glucose. Essential if on medication. |

# 9. The daily routine (about 60 seconds of logging)

1. **Wake:** weigh in. Enter it on Today.
2. **Check the day type:** the app suggests Cut, Modern (training) or Carb-up. Accept or change it.
3. **Eat the template.** Tap each meal as eaten. If you ate something else, tap **Off plan** and enter rough macros.
4. **Tick the habits:** vegetables, water, no alcohol.
5. **Evening:** enter steps and sleep (or pull them from Trainer), rate hunger and energy 1–5, and tick any symptoms.

# 10. The weekly check-in

Every Monday the app compares the last 7 days' average weight with the 7 days before and applies these rules in order:

| Situation | Decision |
|---|---|
| Fewer than 12 complete days in the last 14 | Keep logging. No changes. |
| Strength fell in two comparable sessions | Review sleep and deficit. Add 100–150 kcal or deload. |
| Losing over 1%/week, and tired | Add 100–200 kcal or drop one cardio session. |
| Losing over 1%/week, feeling fine | No change; re-check next week. |
| Losing 0.5–1.0%/week | On track. No change. |
| Losing 0.3–0.5%/week | Acceptable, especially later in the cut. No change. |
| Under 0.3%/week, waist down 0.5 cm or more | Hold one more week. |
| Under 0.3%/week, adherence under 85% | Tighten execution. Don't cut calories. |
| Under 0.3%/week, adherence 85% or more | Remove 100–150 kcal or add about 1,500 steps a day. One change only. |

The same rules are in `data/decision-rules.json`.

# 11. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| **Constipation** | More vegetables, 5–10 g psyllium with a big glass of water, 3 litres of fluid. Fewer strict days. |
| **Hungry all the time** | Bigger vegetable portions, more egg whites, the top-up at your hungriest time. Check sleep. |
| **No appetite (medication)** | Eat the protein first. Use liquid protein (whey, yogurt) when solids are hard. Don't skip meals. |
| **Nausea after big fatty meals** | Smaller, leaner meals (swap pork patties for 5% beef, fewer yolks). Tell your prescriber. |
| **Training feels flat** | Carb-up the day before training; shorten the clock to 72 hours. |
| **Weight jumped overnight** | Carb-up, salty meal, poor sleep or a hard session. Look at the 7-day average. |
| **Stalled for 2+ weeks** | Let the weekly rules decide. Check adherence honestly first. |
| **Went off plan** | Log it, eat the next meal on plan. No compensation fasting. |
| **Alcohol** | Not part of the cut. If you drink, log it; it counts as off plan. |
| **Bad blood test (LDL up a lot)** | Leaner meat, fewer yolks (use whites), more fish and olive oil. Discuss with your GP. |

# 12. Red flags: stop and get help

| Symptom | Action |
|---|---|
| Severe or persistent abdominal pain, possibly spreading to the back | Urgent medical help (NHS 111, or 999 if severe) |
| Repeated vomiting or can't keep fluids down | NHS 111 today |
| Fainting, chest pain, racing or irregular heartbeat | 999 |
| Yellow skin or eyes, or pain under the right ribs after fatty meals | GP promptly |
| Dizzy on standing, very dark urine or not passing urine | Rehydrate, get medical advice |
| No bowel movement for 4+ days with pain or bloating | Pharmacist or GP |

The app shows these as soon as you tick a matching symptom.

# 13. Medication

Retatrutide is an investigational drug and isn't approved in the UK or the US. This guide and the app never advise on dose, timing, escalation or where to get it. If you take it:

- Tell your GP or a prescriber, and get bloods checked.
- Hit your protein target every day regardless of appetite.
- Avoid very large, very fatty meals if they make nausea worse.
- No extended fasts unless a clinician agrees.
- Log what you take and any symptoms in the app, so you have a record to show a clinician.

# 14. Reaching the goal

At 75 kg, take photos, measure your waist and compare your strength with week 1 (a DEXA scan is worth it here). Then choose:

- **Continue to 70 kg** if visible fat remains over the lower abs and lower back and strength is within about 10% of your best.
- **Stop and maintain** if you are already lean and strength is falling.

**Moving to maintenance:** add 100–200 kcal a week (mostly carbohydrates) until your 7-day average is stable for 2 weeks. Keep protein at 150 g+, both training sessions and your step count. Expect 1–2 kg to come back as glycogen and water. That is fine.
